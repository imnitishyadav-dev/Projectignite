Delicious Pizza Recipe:-

About Pizza

Pizza is one of the most popular foods in the world. It originated in Italy and is loved for its crispy crust, delicious tomato sauce, melted cheese, and a variety of tasty toppings. You can customize pizza with vegetables, chicken, mushrooms, olives, or your favorite ingredients. It is perfect for family dinners, parties, and special occasions, making every bite enjoyable and satisfying.

Ingredients:

For the Dough:

  • 2 cups all-purpose flour
  • 1 teaspoon instant yeast
  • 1 teaspoon sugar
  • ½ teaspoon salt
  • 2 tablespoons olive oil
  • ¾ cup warm water

Pizza Sauce:

  • ½ cup pizza sauce
  • 1 teaspoon oregano
  • ½ teaspoon chili flakes

Toppings:

  • 1 cup mozzarella cheese
  • 1 small onion (sliced)
  • 1 small capsicum (sliced)
  • 1 tomato (sliced)
  • Sweet corn
  • Black olives
  • Mushrooms (optional)

👨‍🍳 Preparation Time

  • Preparation Time: 20 minutes
  • Cooking Time: 15 minutes
  • Total Time: 35 minutes
  • Servings: 2–4 people

Step-by-Step Recipe

Step 1: Prepare the Dough

Mix flour, yeast, sugar, and salt in a bowl. Add warm water and olive oil, then knead the dough until it becomes soft and smooth. Cover it with a cloth and let it rest for about one hour until it doubles in size.

Step 2: Prepare the Base

Place the dough on a flat surface and roll it into a round pizza base. Transfer it to a baking tray or pizza pan.

Step 3: Add the Sauce

Spread pizza sauce evenly over the dough. Sprinkle oregano and chili flakes for extra flavor.

Step 4: Add Cheese and Toppings

Cover the sauce with mozzarella cheese. Add onion, capsicum, tomato, sweet corn, olives, and mushrooms evenly on top.

Step 5: Bake the Pizza

Preheat the oven to 220°C (428°F). Bake the pizza for 12–15 minutes until the crust becomes golden brown and the cheese melts completely.

Step 6: Serve

Remove the pizza from the oven, let it cool for two minutes, slice it into pieces, and serve hot with tomato ketchup or your favorite dipping sauce.

Tips

  • Use fresh mozzarella cheese for better taste.
  • Do not add too much sauce, or the crust may become soggy.
  • Preheat the oven before baking.
  • Add fresh basil leaves after baking for extra flavor.
  • Serve immediately while the cheese is hot and stretchy.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q.Can I make pizza without an oven?

A. Yes. You can cook it in a covered pan or pressure cooker (without the whistle).

Q. Which cheese is best for pizza?

A. Mozzarella cheese is the most popular choice because it melts well and gives a stretchy texture.

Q. Can I use whole wheat flour?

A. Yes. Whole wheat flour is a healthier alternative to all-purpose flour.

Q. How should I store leftover pizza?

A. Store it in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to 2 days and reheat before serving.
